# Cocaína Crash Pattern — Text Message Analysis

After a night of stimulant use (cocaine), the comedown produces a characteristic pattern in text messages. Recognizing this prevents misinterpreting drug crash as genuine emotional content.

## Crash timeline

| Hours after last use | Phase | Symptoms in text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2h | High | Energetic, scattered, rapid-fire audio/messages |
| 3–6h | Plateau/Crash begins | Irritability, defensiveness, tone shift |
| 6–12h | Acute crash | "Vazio", "sem forças", "desesperada", "não consigo chorar" |
| 12–24h | Recovery | Shame, regret, "vergonha de tudo" |

## Key phrases (Portuguese, from real case)

| She said | What it really is |
|---|---|
| "Vazio que não passa por nada" | Dopamine depletion — brain chemistry, not heartbreak |
| "Sem forças, muito estranha" | Physical exhaustion + anhedonia |
| "Desesperada" | Rebound anxiety |
| "Não consigo nem chorar" | Emotional blunting — the tears won't come because the brain is depleted |
| "Vergonha de tudo isso" | Beginning of recovery — self-awareness returning |

## How to respond during crash

1. **DO NOT resolve anything** — No relationship decisions, no forgiveness, no "te amo"
2. **DO NOT confront** — She can't process it. Wait for sobriety (24h+)
3. **Keep it human and minimal**: "Se cuida hoje. Depois conversamos."
4. **Recognize it's chemical, not emotional** — The desperation is real but temporary
5. **Wait for "Não estou te culpando"** — This phrase marks the transition from crash defensiveness to genuine self-reflection

## Distinguish from genuine distress

| Drug crash | Genuine emotional distress |
|---|---|
| Sudden onset after a night out | Builds over time |
| Physical symptoms dominate (can't move, can't cry) | Emotional content dominates |
| "Vazio" and "estranha" as primary words | Specific feelings described |
| Shame about "tudo" (vague, global) | Shame about specific actions |
| Resolves naturally in 24-48h | Persists and deepens |
